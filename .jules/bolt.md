## 2025-05-15 - [Record Binding Optimization] **Learning:** Iterative `as_tibble` inside a loop for thousands of records is a major performance killer and can lead to `vctrs` type mismatch errors when `NULL`s are present in numeric columns. **Action:** Use `dplyr::bind_rows` on the raw list of records after batch-processing columns that contain `NULL`s to maintain data type consistency and gain ~7x speedup.
